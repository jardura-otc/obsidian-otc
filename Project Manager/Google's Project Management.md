This content focuses on the importance of interpersonal skills for project managers in building relationships with team members and stakeholders.

Interpersonal Skills in Project Management

- Strong interpersonal skills are essential for project managers to influence and motivate their teams without formal authority.
- Key skills include communication, negotiation, conflict mediation, and understanding team members' motivations.

Key Interpersonal Skills

- **Communication**: Involves checking in with teammates and providing constructive feedback on their work.
- **Negotiation**: Helps in reaching compromises, such as adjusting deadlines based on team members' needs.

Influencing Without Authority

- **Conflict Mediation**: Essential for resolving tensions and ensuring project continuity, often requiring meetings to address disagreements.
- **Understanding Motivations**: Knowing what drives team members can enhance motivation and improve team dynamics.

Overall, these interpersonal skills are crucial for effective project management and can be highlighted during job interviews.
